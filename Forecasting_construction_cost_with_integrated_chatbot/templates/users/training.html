Train machine learning models on construction cost data

💡 About Training: This process trains the available models (Random Forest, plus XGBoost where installed) on the construction project dataset, then keeps the most accurate one for predictions. The models learn from area, floors, city, material quality and building type. Material and labour costs are predicted, not given as inputs. Near-perfect scores are expected here because the dataset's rates follow a fixed pattern by city and quality, so expect larger errors on real projects.
